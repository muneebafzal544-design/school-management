# Student User Guide

## Getting Started

### Logging In

1. Open the school portal in your browser
2. On the **Login** page, enter your **username** and **password** (provided by your school admin)
3. Click **Sign In**

> **Forgot your password?** Click "Forgot Password" and enter your registered email address. You will receive a reset link within a few minutes.

Your dashboard loads automatically after login.

---

## Dashboard

The **Student Dashboard** is your home screen. It shows:

| Section | What it shows |
|---------|---------------|
| Attendance Summary | Your present/absent/late counts this month |
| Fee Status | Outstanding invoices, if any |
| Today's Timetable | Your classes for today |
| Upcoming Exams | Exam dates in the next 7 days |
| Pending Homework | Assignments due soon |
| Latest Announcements | Pinned notices from the school |

---

## Attendance

### Viewing Your Attendance

1. Go to **Attendance** from the sidebar
2. Your **monthly attendance** is displayed as a calendar with colour-coded days:
   - 🟢 Green = Present
   - 🔴 Red = Absent
   - 🟡 Yellow = Late
   - ⚪ Grey = Holiday / Weekend
3. At the top, you will see your **attendance percentage** for the current month

### Checking Annual Attendance

- Use the **month selector** at the top to switch between months
- Your overall percentage is displayed in the summary card

---

## Fees

### Viewing Your Fee Status

1. Go to **Fees** from the sidebar
2. All invoices are listed with their status:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Unpaid | Invoice generated, no payment made |
| Partial | Some amount paid, balance remaining |
| Paid | Fully paid |
| Overdue | Due date has passed, not fully paid |

### Downloading a Fee Receipt

1. Click on any **Paid** invoice
2. Click **Download Receipt** to save a PDF copy

### Understanding Your Invoice

Each invoice shows:
- **Total Amount** — base fee amount
- **Fine/Late Fee** — if payment was delayed
- **Discount** — concession applied (if any)
- **Net Payable** = Total + Fine − Discount

---

## Timetable

1. Go to **Timetable** from the sidebar
2. Your weekly timetable is displayed as a grid — periods on rows, days on columns
3. Each cell shows the **subject** and the **teacher's name**
4. Use the **Print** button to print your timetable

---

## Homework

### Viewing Homework

1. Go to **Homework** from the sidebar
2. Pending assignments are listed with:
   - Subject
   - Description
   - Due date

### Submitting Homework

1. Click on a homework assignment
2. Type your answer or upload a file
3. Click **Submit**

> Once submitted, you can view your submission but cannot edit it.

---

## Exams & Results

### Viewing Exam Schedule

1. Go to **Exams** from the sidebar
2. The date sheet shows all upcoming exams with date, time, and subject

### Viewing Your Results

1. Go to **Results** → **My Results**
2. Select the exam from the dropdown
3. Your marks, grade, and position are displayed per subject

### Downloading Your Report Card

1. Go to **Results** → **Report Card**
2. Select the academic term
3. Click **Download PDF**

---

## Announcements

1. Go to **Announcements** in the sidebar
2. All school-wide notices appear here
3. Pinned announcements appear at the top

---

## School Assistant (Chatbot)

The 🤖 **School Assistant** button is available on every page (bottom-right corner).

### What you can ask:

| Question | Example |
|----------|---------|
| Attendance | "What is my attendance this month?" |
| Fees | "Do I have any pending fees?" |
| Timetable | "What class do I have at 10am?" |
| Homework | "What homework is due tomorrow?" |
| Exams | "When is my next exam?" |
| Announcements | "Latest school notices" |

Click a **quick action chip** or type your question directly.

---

## Transport

If you are enrolled in the school transport service:

1. Go to **Transport** from the sidebar
2. View your assigned **bus number**, **route**, and **driver name**
3. Click **Live Track** to see the bus location on a map in real time

---

## Profile & Settings

1. Click your **name/avatar** at the top-right
2. Select **Profile** to view your details
3. Select **Change Password** to update your password

> **Important:** After receiving your initial credentials, change your password immediately.

---

## Mobile App

The school system is available as a mobile app for iOS and Android.

1. Download **SchoolApp** from the App Store or Google Play
2. Log in with the same username and password
3. All features work on mobile including live bus tracking

---

## Getting Help

If you encounter any issue:
- Use the **School Assistant** chatbot for quick questions
- Contact your **class teacher** for academic issues
- Visit the **school office** or call the admin desk for account or fee issues
