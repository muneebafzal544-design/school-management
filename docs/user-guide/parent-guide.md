# Parent User Guide

## Getting Started

### Logging In

1. Open the school portal at your school's website
2. Enter your **username** and **password** (provided when your child was enrolled)
3. Click **Sign In**

> **First time logging in?** You would have received your credentials via SMS or at the school office. If not, contact the admin desk.

---

## Dashboard

Your **Parent Dashboard** gives you a complete overview of your child's school life.

| Section | What it shows |
|---------|---------------|
| Child Summary | Name, class, attendance % for the month |
| Fee Status | Unpaid invoices |
| Latest Announcements | Notices from the school |
| Upcoming Exams | Exams scheduled in the next 7 days |
| Homework | Pending assignments |
| Bus Status | Live location of your child's school bus |

If you have **multiple children** enrolled, you can switch between them using the child selector at the top.

---

## Attendance

### Viewing Your Child's Attendance

1. Go to **Attendance** from the left sidebar
2. A calendar shows each school day colour-coded:
   - 🟢 **Green** = Present
   - 🔴 **Red** = Absent
   - 🟡 **Yellow** = Late
   - ⚪ **Grey** = Holiday / Weekend
3. The summary card shows the **attendance percentage** for the current month

### What to Do If Your Child Was Absent

If your child was absent and you want to submit a leave note:
1. Go to **Attendance**
2. Click **Submit Leave Request** (if available)
3. Enter the reason and date range
4. The teacher will mark it as **Excused**

---

## Fees

### Checking Fee Status

1. Go to **Fees** from the sidebar
2. All invoices are listed with amounts and due dates

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Unpaid | No payment made yet |
| Partial | Some amount paid, balance remaining |
| Paid | Fully cleared |
| Overdue | Due date passed, unpaid |

### Downloading Fee Receipts

1. Click on any **Paid** invoice
2. Click **Download Receipt**
3. Save or print the PDF for your records

### Fee Challans (for bank payments)

1. Go to **Fees** and select the invoice
2. Click **Download Challan**
3. Take the printed challan to your bank for payment
4. The admin will record the payment and update the status

---

## Results & Report Cards

### Viewing Exam Results

1. Go to **Exams / Results** from the sidebar
2. Select the exam or term
3. View marks per subject, grade, and class rank

### Downloading the Report Card

1. Go to **Exams** → **Report Card**
2. Select the academic term
3. Click **Download PDF** for a formatted report card

---

## Homework

1. Go to **Homework** from the sidebar
2. All pending homework assignments are listed by subject and due date
3. Click an assignment to see the full instructions

> This helps you stay on top of your child's academic workload from home.

---

## Announcements

1. Go to **Announcements** from the sidebar
2. All notices from the school appear here — events, holidays, PTM schedules, and important updates
3. Pinned announcements are shown at the top

You also receive **push notifications** on your phone when a new announcement is posted.

---

## Transport / Bus Tracking

If your child uses the school bus:

1. Go to **Transport** from the sidebar
2. See your child's assigned:
   - **Bus number**
   - **Route name and stops**
   - **Driver name and phone**
   - **Pickup and drop time**
3. Click **Live Track** to open the real-time map

### Live Map

The map shows:
- The bus's current location (updates every 30 seconds)
- The school location
- Estimated arrival time

> **Tip:** Use this every morning to know exactly when to have your child ready at the pickup point.

---

## Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTM)

### Viewing Scheduled Meetings

1. Go to **Meetings** from the sidebar
2. Upcoming meetings with teachers are listed with date, time, and agenda

### Requesting a Meeting

1. Click **Request Meeting**
2. Select the teacher, preferred date/time, and topic
3. The teacher receives a notification and confirms

---

## Messaging Teachers

1. Go to **Messages** from the sidebar
2. Select the teacher you want to contact
3. Type your message and click **Send**

> Messages are private between you and the teacher. The admin can view messages only in case of a complaint.

---

## School Assistant (Chatbot)

Use the 🤖 chatbot button (bottom-right corner) to ask quick questions:

| Question | Example |
|----------|---------|
| Attendance | "Was my child present today?" |
| Fees | "Are there any pending fees?" |
| Bus | "Where is the school bus?" |
| Exams | "When is the next exam?" |
| Announcements | "Latest notices" |

---

## Managing Your Account

### Changing Your Password

1. Click your name at the top-right
2. Select **Change Password**
3. Enter your current password and new password
4. Click **Update**

> Use a strong password with at least 8 characters including numbers and symbols.

### Updating Contact Information

For changes to your phone number or email address, contact the **school office** directly.
